# Smelliest Pilgrim

*[Español](es/)*

A voice-memo trail journal on your wrist, for pilgrims and thru-hikers.
Talk to your watch, and every memo keeps the place where you said it.
It works offline and sends your memos only to your own phone or your own server.

- [Source code, downloads and how to use it](https://github.com/smelliestpilgrim/smelliest-pilgrim)
- [Phone app download (latest release)](https://github.com/smelliestpilgrim/smelliest-pilgrim/releases/latest)
- [Privacy statement](privacy.html)
